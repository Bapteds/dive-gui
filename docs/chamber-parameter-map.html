Details

Hover or tap a parameter to trace its links. Toggle a link type in the legend to hide it.

Every parameter has its own fit on the three inputs X1, X2, X3; that fit is the fallback whenever a relation is switched off. A refine link sharpens a fit only when its partner is measured (typed as an Exact). A combination link makes the parameter equal a formula of other parameters' final values. Precedence per parameter: Set exact > relation (if on) > X1–X3 fit → then Min/Max clamp.
