## 2026-10-08 · Fix · Chamber: guide vanes turn the right way, about a spindle inside the blade
**Area**: python, tests, docs · **Commits**: branch `fix/vane-pitch-spindle` (from `main` at `a58c228`), not committed
**Summary**: Found while building a standalone 33° vane ring for the user (Hristo). Two defects in how `make_vane_patches` turns each blade to `vaneAngleDeg`: (1) the pitch `vaneAngleDeg − 50` turned the blades counter-clockwise for larger angles, so 45° closed the ring and 55° opened it, while the user's convention is **smaller = more open**; (2) the spindle took `pivotRadius` (right) at the azimuth of the **mean mesh vertex** (wrong): the leading edge's dense tessellation dragged it 3.6° off, to (0.27948, −0.82106) m, **15 mm outside the blade**, so a pitched blade slid about 4.7 mm at ±5° instead of turning in place, and blades above 16 vanes shrank towards that outside point. Now the pitch is `50 − vaneAngleDeg` (counter-clockwise) and the spindle is the mid-thickness point of the pivot circle inside the blade.
**Details**:
- `buildChamber.py`: new pure helpers `_vane_pitch_deg(vane_angle_deg)` (`VANE_BASE_ANGLE_DEG − angle`) and `_vane_spindle_xy(np, loop, radius)` (circle / closed-outline intersection, mid-arc point, `ValueError` unless exactly two crossings); `make_vane_patches` reads the spindle on the asset blade's mid-height section (asset: (0.32992, −0.80212) m, azimuth −67.64°, about 28 mm inside the outline, about 50 % chord) and scales it with the ring; `main()` passes `_vane_pitch_deg(vane_angle)`. Comments of `VANE_BASE_ANGLE_DEG`, `VANE_MIN_GAP` and the blade-gap guard updated (smallest gap now ≈ 0.51 chord at 16, 0.52 at 32, 1.18 at 8, all at 55°).
- Unchanged: every build at 50° with up to 16 vanes (no pitch, no chord scaling: the spindle is not used), so the 16-vane GOLDEN volumes do not move; `stepped-vanes-18` stays within `VOL_RTOL`. Changed on purpose: angles other than 50° (opposite direction, new centre) and counts above 16 (new chord-scaling centre).
- Range 45..55 and the build key unchanged; saves are not migrated (a saved 45° now builds more open, 55° more closed).
- The true CAD spindle is still unknown: `GuideVanes50Deg.STEP` (user's Desktop, `Empirical Relation/guide vanes/`) holds only surfaces and the 26° SLDPRT could not be read. Mid-thickness on `pivotRadius` stands in (on that circle the blade only spans 45 to 56 % of the chord).
- Doc comments only: `ChamberInput.vaneAngleDeg` (`packages/shared`), `chamber.schemas.ts`, `chamberForm.ts`. The UI helper text of Vane angle is unchanged (it states no direction).
- **Cache**: local `apps/api/storage/chamber/*` purged (19 entries). **On deploy purge `<STORAGE_DIR>/chamber/*` on the server** (the hash does not cover code).
- Side deliverable (not in the repo): a standalone 33° ring for the user's machine (Guide vanes Ø 1735 mm, HLE 563 mm, Runner Ø 1310 mm, outlet ratio 0.45; LE Ø 2223 mm from HLE), made with the fixed builder's own pipeline (`make_vane_patches` → `_vane_prisms` → each prism clipped between the shroud floor and the hub) because 33° is outside the app's 45..55 range (`guideVanes_33deg.stl` on the user's Desktop, ring axis at the origin, z = 0 at the runner case top). Checked against a real build of that machine at 45° (stepped-vanes box with the user's vane inputs): the app's `guide_vanes` patch and the script's vanes agree to 0.001 mm in outline at every height and 0.2 mm at the lowest point over the shroud fillet. At 33° the outlet outer radius is clamped from 655 to 653.3 mm (trailing edges move in to R_anchor 673.6 mm) and the trailing ends reach 32 mm below the runner case top.
