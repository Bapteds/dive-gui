## 2026-09-30 · Fix · Chamber: hub shoulder knee derived from the P1–P3 quarter ellipse

**Summary**: User instruction (Hristo, decisions taken in a Claude Code session): the guide-vane hub shoulder folded back at large Runner Ø (P1 overtook the half-rate P2 from ≈ 2179 mm at ratio 0.45, ≈ 1961 mm at 0.50) and at the small end (P2 past P3 below ≈ 700 mm).
